# Area 7 Challenge Mod — Player Guide

**Version 2.2.5** | By Frilioth | [twitch.tv/frilioth](https://twitch.tv/frilioth)

---

## What Is Area 7?

Area 7 is a challenge mod for 7 Days to Die. You wake up inside a top-secret military research facility surrounded by lethal radiation. Your only goal: build an emergency transmitter, survive a blood moon horde, and escape to Camp Frilsville before the facility kills you.

This is a self-contained challenge. Everything you need is inside the facility. Death is permanent — if you die, the run is over.

---

## Getting Started

When you load into the game, you'll spawn at the gates of Area 7. A survival briefing will appear explaining the situation. Read it carefully.

Your radiation protection (Fallout Flush) is almost spent. The area surrounding the facility is lethally irradiated — stepping outside without protection means death. The facility itself is your only safe zone.

### Recommended Game Settings

- **Air Drops:** Disabled (vanilla airdrops can't be reached)
- **Loot Respawn:** 5 days (or any value except Disabled)
- **Biome Progression:** Off

## The Challenge

The challenge is split into four phases, tracked through the in-game challenge journal.

### Phase 1 — Clear the Area

Kill 10 zombies on the surface. Zombie soldiers patrol what was once a secure perimeter. Clear them before heading underground — or they'll follow you down.

### Phase 2 — Build the Transmitter

Scavenge the facility and gather the materials needed to build an Emergency Transmitter at a workbench:

- **125 Forged Steel** — Smelt iron in a forge. Search workbenches and tool cabinets.
- **50 Mechanical Parts** — Wrench vehicles, air conditioners, and machinery.
- **50 Electrical Parts** — Pull from computers, junction boxes, and electronics.
- **5 Car Batteries** — Search vehicles, storage rooms, and utility areas.
- **20 Duct Tape** — Found in containers and on zombies.

You'll need a forge early — it's essential for smelting forged steel. The hardened military chest at the end of the bunker always contains a crucible, which you'll need to smelt iron into steel.

Once you have the materials, craft the Emergency Transmitter at a workbench.

### Phase 3 — Deploy the Transmitter

Place the transmitter above ground (Y 44 or higher). This is the point of no return.

When you deploy the transmitter:

- A **blood moon horde** is triggered at **10pm that night**
- Dormant sections of the facility reactivate — expect more zombies underground
- A **supply airdrop** arrives at **4am the following morning**
- **Camp Frilsville** coordinates appear on your map as a waypoint
- **Trader Hugh** spawns at Camp Frilsville

Secure a defensive position before activating. Prepare defences, or find somewhere to hide. Your choice.

### Phase 4 — Escape

Make your way to Camp Frilsville by any means necessary. Find Trader Hugh to collect your Extraction Order and complete the challenge.

Check your map waypoints for the Camp Frilsville location.

## The Airdrop

The supply airdrop arrives at 4am the morning after you deploy the transmitter. It contains a Radiation Respirator (allowing you to travel through the irradiated zone) and potentially a vehicle.

Your vehicle depends on how many Grease Monkey (vehicle) magazines you've read:

| Magazines Read | Possible Vehicles | IOU Chance |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 (Tier 1) | Bicycle (50%) | 50% |
| 5–19 (Tier 2) | Bicycle (35%), Minibike (35%) | 30% |
| 20–29 (Tier 3) | Motorcycle (50%), Minibike (30%) | 20% |
| 30–39 (Tier 4) | 4x4 Truck (50%), Motorcycle (35%) | 15% |
| 40+ (Tier 5) | Gyrocopter (40%), 4x4 Truck (30%), Motorcycle (20%) | 8% |

Every tier has a chance at a vehicle. The more magazines you read, the better the vehicle pool and the lower your chance of getting an IOU instead.

If you're unlucky and receive an IOU instead of a vehicle, you can always craft your own as a backup, or make a Fallout Flush Smoothie if you don't want to wait for the airdrop.

**Tip:** Read every magazine you find. Vehicle magazines are your ticket to a better escape.

## Zombie Loot Bags

Zombies inside the facility have a chance to drop themed loot bags when killed. There are eight types:

- **Medical Supplies Bag**
- **Heavy Equipment Bag**
- **Hazmat Supplies Bag**
- **Book Bag**
- **Police Evidence Bag**
- **Researcher's Remains**
- **Biker's Loot Bag**
- **VIP Loot Bag**

These contain supplies tailored to their type. Keep killing — every bag helps.

---

## Radiation

The area outside the facility is lethally irradiated. Radiation damage starts immediately and escalates rapidly the longer you're exposed. Without protection, you will die.

There are two forms of radiation protection:

- **Radiation Respirator** — Included in the airdrop after transmitter deployment
- **Fallout Flush Smoothie** — Craftable temporary protection

If you die and respawn, you will spawn outside the facility in the irradiated zone. There is no surviving this — the radiation will kill you. Your run is over.

---

## Death

Death is permanent. When you die:

- Your run ends immediately
- All progress (challenges, scheduled events, waypoints) is cleaned up
- A mission debrief is saved with your run statistics
- You respawn in the irradiated zone outside the facility — there is no coming back

---

## Mission Debrief

After every run (whether you escape or die), a detailed stats page is saved to:

```
Mods/FrilArea7Challenge/stats/Area7_Debrief.html
```

Open it in any web browser to see your run breakdown, including:

- Total zombie kills with breakdown by type
- Weapons used
- Loot bags dropped with distribution chart
- Drop rate percentage
- Airdrop tier and contents (if opened)
- Mission timeline showing how long each phase took
- Game settings (difficulty, zombie speeds, loot respawn)
- Survivor stats (level, gamestage, peak infection)

The debrief overwrites each run, so your most recent attempt is always available.

## Tips

- **Build a forge early.** You need forged steel for the transmitter and a crucible to make it.
- **Read every magazine.** Your vehicle tier depends on Grease Monkey magazines read.
- **Explore the whole facility.** Resources are spread throughout — offices have electrical parts, vehicles have mechanical parts, storage rooms have batteries.
- **Prepare before deploying the transmitter.** Once you activate it, the blood moon is coming and more zombies wake up underground. Have a plan.
- **Don't wait around.** After the airdrop arrives at 4am, grab your gear and head for Camp Frilsville. The longer you stay, the more dangerous it gets.
- **Check your map.** Camp Frilsville coordinates appear as a waypoint after transmitter deployment.
- **Loot respawns.** Containers restock after the configured respawn period, so revisit areas if you need more supplies.
